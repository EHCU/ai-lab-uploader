ai-lab uploader

A private publishing tool for three YouTube channels owned by Reha Yavuz.

Privacy policy · Terms of Service

What it is

ai-lab uploader is a command-line tool that runs on its owner's own computer. It uploads finished videos to the owner's own YouTube channels using the YouTube Data API v3:

  • All Weapons Hot (@AllWeaponsHot)
  • The Chapter They Skipped (@TheChapterTheySkipped)
  • The Next Move (@TheNextMove-d6j)

What it does

  • Uploads a video file with its title, description, tags, category, language and publish time.
  • Sets "made for kids" to No and declares altered or synthetic content where it applies.
  • Uploads the video's English caption file and custom thumbnail.
  • Checks that it is signed in to the intended channel before uploading anything.

Who can use it

Only its owner. It is not offered to the public, has no other users, no website login and no paid plans. It does not read, store, analyse or display any other YouTube data.
